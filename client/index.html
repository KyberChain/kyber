faraday · the quantum upgrade

One chain, built to outlive elliptic curves.

Faraday is the Kyber upgrade path. Payment detection is already post-quantum on kybq1 addresses. Next, spends move to hash-based proofs and every spend hides in the whole chain.

01 · livePost-quantum payment detection

Quantum addresses carry an ML-KEM-768 key. Senders encapsulate to it, so only the receiver can find and open the payment. A quantum computer cannot.

02 · faradayFull-chain privacy

Today a spend hides among 16 outputs. After Faraday it hides among every output on the chain.

03 · faradayPost-quantum spends

Ring signatures move from elliptic curves to hash-based proofs, so nobody can forge a spend with a quantum computer.

04 · liveMemory-hard mining

Argon2id proof of work. Memory-hard, so ordinary CPUs stay competitive and ASIC farms gain less.

05 · liveWallet in your browser

Keys are made and kept on your device. Your browser scans the chain, builds the ring signature and range proofs, then submits.

06 · faradaySame chain, no lost funds

No new coin, no relaunch. Your wallet moves your coins to the new format once, and it does the work for you.
