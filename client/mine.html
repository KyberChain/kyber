Mine Kyber

open mining · argon2id · rewards to your address

Kyber Chain blocks are open to every miner. Your browser or CPU solves Argon2id, a memory-hard proof of work that favours ordinary computers over specialised hardware. When you find a block, the reward is paid straight to your own private address as a coinbase output only your wallet can spend.

The miner fetches work from GET /api/work?address=… and submits solutions to POST /api/work. The node re-checks every hash before it accepts a block. Point --node at your own node to mine against it.
